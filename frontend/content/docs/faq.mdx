<Accordions type="multiple">
  <Accordion title="Who can take part?">
    You must be a teenager aged **13–18** (inclusive).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I am a complete beginner. Can I still participate?">
    Of course! Torque is designed to be a hands-on way to get started with hardware and motors. You'll have support from the community in Slack whenever you run into blockers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is this really free?">
    Yes! We cover the parts for you to build your project, plus opportunities to earn extra prizes. You can read more about how Hack Club grants work at [hackclub.com](https://hackclub.com).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What kind of projects can I build?">
    Any hardware project that uses a motor qualifies. Some ideas:
    
    * RC cars or autonomous rovers
    * Self-balancing platforms
    * Custom 3D printers or CNC plotters
    * Motorized blinds or smart door locks
    
    If it incorporates a motor into the physical mechanism, you can build it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do we track time?">
    All time spent designing and building goes through our time-tracking tools:

    <Cards>
      <Card
        title="Hackatime"
        href="https://hackatime.hackclub.com/"
        description="Tracks coding time directly inside your code editor."
      />
      <Card
        title="Lapse"
        href="https://lapse.hackclub.com/"
        description="Captures screen time for CAD, PCB design, or bench work."
      />
    </Cards>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the funding limit?">
    There is no set grant ceiling—funding scales directly with your design time at a fixed rate of **$5/hour** (for example, 40 hours = a $200 grant).
    
    > **Note:** The calculated total represents your maximum spending limit. Always budget conservatively and aim below your cap.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are Volts?">
    **Volts** are Torque’s shop currency. You earn them by shipping your completed project, and you can redeem them for hardware gear and prizes in the shop.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use AI in my project?">
    Yes, in moderation. AI is acceptable for brainstorming, debugging, or editing, but it cannot design or code the core project for you. 
    
    Most of the hardware and software must be your own original work, and any AI assistance must be disclosed in your project submission.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
