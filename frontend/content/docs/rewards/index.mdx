Torque gives you two separate rewards, at two different points in your project:

1. **Hardware funding (before you build).** When you ship your design, you get a grant of **$5/hr** to buy the parts. This is only on the funding path.
2. **Volts (after you build).** When you ship your finished, working build, your hours turn into **Volts** to spend in the shop on motors, ESCs, and other prizes.

Each hour pays out once: either as grant money or as Volts, not both. On the no-funding path there's no grant, so every hour becomes Volts.

| Level | Base | With a full streak |

| **1** | 35 Volts/hr | 40 Volts/hr |
| **2** | 40 Volts/hr | 50 Volts/hr |
| **3** | 45 Volts/hr | 55 Volts/hr |
| **4** | 55 Volts/hr | 65 Volts/hr |

Your level is set by reviewers. See [Levels](/docs/projects/levels) for what each one means.

## Streaks

Every day your journals cover **at least 1 hour** adds **+0.01×** to your project's multiplier, up to your level's max. Working on your project a little every day beats cramming it all at the end.

- **Each project has its own streak.** A day runs midnight to midnight in the timezone you picked when you signed up.
- **Streak freezes.** You start with **3**, shared across your projects. If you miss a day, a freeze is used automatically and your streak holds. With none left, the streak resets to zero. You can turn freezes off for a project if you'd rather save them.
- **Paused during review.** While a ship is waiting on review, missed days don't count against you.
- **Reminders.** If you're in the Slack, Torque DMs you at 8 PM your time when today still needs its hour.

---

## Grant or Volts?

On the funding path you choose how many hours to use for your grant. A few things to think about:

- **Use what you need to build it.** The whole point is getting your project built, so budget carefully and conservatively.
- **Leftover hours aren't wasted.** Anything you don't use becomes Volts when you ship the working build.

---

## When you get paid

Every ship is reviewed, then gets a final approval. Grants and Volts are only paid out after the final approval:

- **Volts** land in your balance right away.
- **Grants** are sent to you by an organizer. You can't ship your build until your grant has been sent.

---

## The shop

Volts are spent in the Torque shop on motor-related prizes like motors, ESCs, drivers, and other cool prizes. The shop stays open until **January 6, 2027** (US Eastern), a week after the last builds are due, so spend your Volts before then.
