You build something you're proud of, and Torque helps you make it real: **grants** to fund your build, and **Volts** to spend in the shop on motors, ESCs, and other prizes.

<div className="grid gap-4 sm:grid-cols-2">
    <Callout type="success" title="Grants">
      On the funding path, your design hours turn into a grant to buy the parts for your project, including a v2 if you need one.
    </Callout>
    <Callout type="idea" title="Volts">
      Once you ship a working build, the hours you didn't use for a grant turn into Volts. How many depends on your level and streak.
    </Callout>
</div>

## Volts by level

| Level | Base | With a full streak |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **1** | 35 Volts/hr | 40 Volts/hr |
| **2** | 40 Volts/hr | 50 Volts/hr |
| **3** | 45 Volts/hr | 55 Volts/hr |
| **4** | 55 Volts/hr | 65 Volts/hr |

Your level is set by reviewers. See [Levels](/docs/projects/levels) for what each one means.

---

## Streaks

Every day you log **at least 1 hour** adds **+0.01×** to your project's multiplier, up to your level's max. Working on your project a little every day beats cramming it all at the end.

---

## Grant or Volts?

On the funding path you choose how many hours to use for your grant. A few things to think about:

- **Use what you need to build it.** The whole point is getting your project built, so budget carefully and conservatively.
- **Leftover hours aren't wasted.** Anything you don't use becomes Volts when you ship the working build.

---

## The shop

Volts are spent in the Torque shop on motor-related prizes like motors, ESCs, drivers, and other cool prizes.
