## What is Torque?

Torque is a Hack Club **You Ship, We Ship** for hardware that moves.

- **You Ship:** any hardware project that uses a motor.
- **We Ship:** funding to build it, plus motor-related prizes like motors, ESCs, and drivers.

A 3D printer? Yep. A mini elevator? Yep! A self-balancing robot, a camera slider, a motorized desk flag? As long as a motor makes it move, it counts.

<Callout type="idea" title="Why motors?">
  Moving things is arguably the most important physical use of electricity in the modern world, from EVs to the car in your driveway. And projects that *move* just feel more real than any other kind of hardware.
</Callout>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Design your project
    Come up with a motor project you actually want to exist, then design it: CAD, wiring, PCB, firmware. Track your time with Hackatime & Lapse.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Journal as you go
    Post journal entries about the ups, the downs, and the in-betweens. They're how reviewers verify your hours.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Ship it
    Publish your design so anyone could rebuild it. On the funding path, claim your design hours as a **$5/hr grant** to buy parts.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Build it and ship again
    Build the real thing, keep journaling, and ship the working project. Any unclaimed hours turn into **Volts** for the shop.
  </Step>
</Steps>
