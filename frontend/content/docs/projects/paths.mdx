Every Torque project follows one of two paths. Both are hardware projects built around a motor; the difference is **when you ship** and **how your hours get paid out**.

| | Funding path | No-funding path |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Best for** | You need parts to build your design | You already have the parts (or are paying yourself) |
| **When you first ship** | Once the design is done | Once the build is done and working |
| **Grant** | $5/hr for the hours you claim | None |
| **Volts** | Only for hours you *don't* claim as grant | For every hour |
| **Must show a working build?** | Yes, at your final ship | Yes, at your only ship |

---

<Tabs items={["Funding path", "No-funding path"]}>
  <Tab value="Funding path">
    ### Design first, get a grant, then build

    <Steps>
      <Step>
        **Design your project.** CAD, schematics, PCB, firmware, and a BOM, all presentable and documented. Track every hour.
      </Step>
      <Step>
        **Ship the design.** Submit it and claim hours for a grant at **$5/hr**. You don't have to claim all of them; hours you leave unclaimed can become Volts later.
      </Step>
      <Step>
        **Build it.** Order your parts and assemble the project, still tracking time and journaling as you go.
      </Step>
      <Step>
        **Need more?** If something needs fixing (a v2 PCB, a stronger motor, a redesigned mount), you can claim more of your hours as grant.
      </Step>
      <Step>
        **Ship the finished build.** Show it working. Any hours you never claimed as grant are converted into **Volts** at your level's rate.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="No-funding path">
    ### Build it yourself, turn every hour into Volts

    <Steps>
      <Step>
        **Design and build your project** using parts you already have or buy yourself, tracking time and journaling the whole way.
      </Step>
      <Step>
        **Ship the completed, working build.** You only ship once, and it has to work.
      </Step>
      <Step>
        **All of your approved hours** are converted into **Volts** at your level's rate.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout type="info" title="Grants vs. Volts">
  Grants are a flat **$5/hr** no matter your level. Volts depend on your [project level](/docs/projects/levels) and your streak, and higher levels can be worth more per hour than the grant.
</Callout>
