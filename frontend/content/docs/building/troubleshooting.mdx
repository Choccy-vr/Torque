You're going to run into a *lot* of problems while building. That's normal, and it's also great journal material.

## General tips

- **Search it up first.** Chances are someone else has hit the exact same problem.
- **Write down what you've tried.** It helps you find the problem, and it makes getting help much easier.
- **Change one thing at a time.** If you change three things and it works, you won't know which one fixed it.
- **Isolate it.** Test the motor alone with a minimal sketch before blaming your full firmware.
- **Don't give up.** Never give up on fixing something; there is almost always another way. IF you truly can't find a fix, take a break from it for a bit. As a last resort, you could always work for a v2
## Asking for help

Sometimes searching turns up nothing and you're stuck. That's what the community is for! Post in [#torque](https://hackclub.enterprise.slack.com/archives/C0BHGT33JHH) or #hardware, etc

Here are a few steps for good questions

<Steps>
  <Step>**Context:** what you're building and what you're trying to do right now.</Step>
  <Step>**The problem:** what you expected vs. what actually happens.</Step>
  <Step>**What you've tried** already, and what each attempt did.</Step>
  <Step>**Photos/videos** of the wiring and the problem, plus your schematic if you have one.</Step>
  <Step>**Links:** your GitHub repo and the part numbers or datasheets involved.</Step>
</Steps>
