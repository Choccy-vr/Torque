When you're picking parts, **be resourceful**. Make the most of every dollar instead of spending your whole grant just because you can. Every dollar saved goes toward your prizes and the next person's project.

If you're ever unsure about your BOM, post it in [#torque](https://hackclub.enterprise.slack.com/archives/C0BHGT33JHH). People can help you pick better parts before you buy.

<div className="grid gap-4 sm:grid-cols-2">
  <Callout type="success" title="Do">
    - Research which parts to use
    - Reuse proven motor + driver combos from other projects
    - Ask other communities for advice
    - Order early and budget for shipping
  </Callout>
  <Callout type="error" title="Don't">
    - Buy tools (like a $100 oscilloscope) with your grant
    - Buy extra parts "for later projects"
    - Pay $40 on Amazon for a module that's $5 on AliExpress if you can wait
  </Callout>
</div>

Everything you buy should end up in your **BOM**, with links to the exact part.

## AliExpress tips

AliExpress has a high skill ceiling. A few things to know:

- The **welcome deal only works once**. Don't budget around it; prices go up afterwards.
- **Check the shipping cost.** A lower price with paid shipping is often more expensive than a slightly higher price with free shipping.
- The **estimated delivery date** is usually fairly accurate. Plan around it.
- Check that the listing variant you picked is the right one; many listings bundle several different parts.
