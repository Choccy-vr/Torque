<Callout title="Before you start">
  You'll need a Hack Club account and a [Hackatime](https://hackatime.hackclub.com) account to track your hours.
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create a project

Head to **Projects** and give your project a title and a short description.

</Step>
<Step>

### Log devlogs as you build

Post a devlog whenever you make progress: what you did, what broke, and pictures.

</Step>
<Step>

### Ship it

When your design is ready, ship it for review. A reviewer will approve it or ask for changes.

</Step>
</Steps>
