Journaling is how you keep track of your progress while you work on your project: short write-ups of what you did, backed by photos and the time you spent.

It isn't just for reviewers. Long after you've finished, your journal is the story of how the thing got built: the wrong motor you ordered, the driver you fried, the moment it finally moved. It's also a great way to share your journey with people who might want to build something similar.
